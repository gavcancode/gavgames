## Licensing and hosting notes

- The sausi-7/games repository declares MIT. Preserve the copyright and license notice if copying or redistributing substantial portions of its code.
- HexGL's README describes its code/resources as MIT unless a file says otherwise. Preserve file-specific notices and credits.
- Hextris is GPL-3.0-or-later. If GavGames copies, modifies, and distributes its code, comply with GPL requirements, including making corresponding source available under the applicable terms.
- A Dark Room is MPL-2.0. If GavGames copies and distributes covered files, comply with MPL source-disclosure requirements for those files.
- The 19 third-party titles are currently embedded from upstream hosts, not copied into this repository. Upstream availability and iframe permissions can change. The GavGames player provides a direct-open fallback.
- The 15 sausi-7 titles were selected from distinct source HTML files after duplicate/mislabelled entries were discovered during review. Their source files and relative paths were checked against the upstream repository tree. This is a source-integrity check, not a full manual gameplay test.
- Before self-hosting any third-party game, re-check the exact version's license, asset/audio/font notices, trademarks, and required attributions.
